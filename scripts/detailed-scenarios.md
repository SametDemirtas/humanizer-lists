# Detailed request scenarios (master, English)

This is the brief given to the model that drafted the requests in `scripts/detailed-requests/`.

Each scenario becomes TWO prompts per language, written as a real person would type them to ChatGPT or Claude:
- A (quick): one or two sentences with the key facts, casual, informal tone.
- B (careful): a fuller prompt with audience, tone, length, and the facts as a list or notes; often asks for a text that is ready to send or post.
Vary phrasing between scenarios. Some B prompts paste rough notes ("my notes: ...") and ask to turn them into the text. Some say "no placeholders", "don't add a title", "write it as me", "keep it under 150 words", "informal", "formal". Never mention AI detection, humanizing, tells, style lists, or "sound human". Do not ask for Markdown or for no Markdown unless it is natural for that person (e.g. "it's for WhatsApp/email, plain text").
Localise names, places, currency, dates and institutions to the language's country (it: Italy, es: Spain for half and Mexico/Argentina/Colombia for half, nl: Netherlands and some Flanders, de: Germany and some Austria/Switzerland). Keep the facts' substance.

## blog
1. Personal blog: I ran my first half marathon last Sunday in 2:11 after six months of training; knee pain in March nearly made me quit; my physio's advice; next goal a 10K under 50 min.
2. Food blog: recipe post for my grandmother's lemon cake; she made it every Easter; I changed the sugar amount; tips about the oven.
3. Travel blog: 5 days hiking in the mountains (it: Dolomites, es: Picos de Europa / Mexico: Nevado de Toluca, nl: Ardennes, de: Harz/Allgäu) with my sister; we slept in huts; rain on day 3; best moment.
4. DIY blog: we redid our bathroom ourselves in 3 weekends; cost about 2,400 (local currency); two mistakes we made (grout, silicone); what we'd do differently.
5. Small business blog (bakery owner): we start gluten-free bread from Monday; it took 8 months of tests; price 4.50; free tasting on Saturday morning.
6. Parenting blog: our 11-year-old got her first smartphone; the rules we agreed on as a family; what worked after two months.
7. Gardening blog: my first year with a plot in the community garden; tomatoes failed, beans were great; what I learned about watering.
8. Book blog: review of a novel I just finished (any real well-known novel from that country's literature, published before 2020); what I liked, what dragged.
